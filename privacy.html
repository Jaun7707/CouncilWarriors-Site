YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

Privacy policy.

Effective October 28, 2026 · Council Warriors version 3.0

Council Warriors is developed by Joseph Hoffmann. This policy explains how the game and this support website handle information. The game does not require a player account. Optional gameplay analytics and crash reporting are off until you choose to share.

Local gameplay data

The game stores settings, match setup, tutorial progress, compatible historical unlocks and purchase-related state on your device. Deleting the app can remove local saves. StoreKit’s verified purchase history is separate from those saves and is used to establish access to purchased content.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and restoration. The game receives product and transaction information needed to verify an unlock, including purchase or revocation status. It does not receive your payment-card details. If you opt into analytics, verified purchase information is also sent to Google Analytics to help us understand purchases and improve the game.

Optional analytics and crash reports

If you choose Share & Improve, Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process:

  • Gameplay interactions such as selected fighters and arenas, settings, tutorial progress, match outcomes and summarized combat activity.
  • App version, device and operating-system information, session activity and performance measurements.
  • Crash reports, error traces and diagnostic context that help identify defects.
  • App-installation identifiers and purchase information. These records can be associated with an installation; they are not anonymous simply because the game does not ask for a name.

Google Analytics may derive approximate geography from network information. The game does not request precise location, contacts, photos or health data. We do not add your name, email address or Apple Account identifier to custom gameplay events.

No advertising tracking

The game has no advertisements or advertising SDK. Advertising identifier collection is not included, IDFV collection is disabled, and advertising consent, personalization and advertising data sharing are denied in the app’s Firebase configuration. Gameplay measurement is used for product improvement and diagnostics, not cross-app advertising tracking.

Your choices

Play whether or not you share. Change your choice in Settings → Privacy. Choosing Don’t Share stops future application analytics, resets local Analytics data and deletes unsent crash reports and pending local purchase measurements. It does not recall reports already delivered to Google or erase Apple’s purchase records.

Providers, retention and deletion

Google processes the optional analytics and diagnostics, and may process data outside your country. Analytics retention follows the project’s Google Analytics settings; exported analytics tables currently expire after 60 days. Aggregated reports can have different retention. Firebase states that Crashlytics crash data and associated identifiers are kept for 90 days before removal begins. See Firebase’s privacy and security information and Google’s privacy policy.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact us through stridequests.support@gmail.com. We do not maintain a player account that lets us identify every installation from a person’s name; we may need additional information to locate relevant records. Do not send sensitive information in a public post. Rights and available remedies depend on your location, and you may contact your local data-protection authority.

Support messages and this website

If you contact us, we use the information you voluntarily provide to answer your question and troubleshoot. This website is a static GitHub Pages site without our own analytics scripts, tracking pixels or contact form. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement. External support links operate under their providers’ policies.

Children and families

No account, chat or public profile is offered in the game. Analytics is optional for every player. A parent or guardian can contact us about information they believe was shared by a child, and we will review the request. Consult the current App Store age rating for the game’s content.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date. Questions about privacy or the game can be sent to Joseph Hoffmann through stridequests.support@gmail.com.
