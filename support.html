Purchases and saves

What is included in version 3.0?

Version 3.0 includes Rick, Lionel, Mike and MMeM, plus Jungle Path and Whispering Willow, free. The tutorial, all CPU difficulty levels, item options and matches against one or two CPUs are included. One non-consumable Full Game purchase unlocks all 24 launch fighters and ten launch arenas immediately.

How do I restore Full Game?

In version 3.0, open Full Game from the home menu and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that purchased the unlock and an internet connection. Apple manages your purchase history. Full Game is a permanent purchase, not a subscription.

A purchase is pending, failed or cancelled

Pending purchases may need approval through Apple. Cancelling does not purchase the game. If a purchase fails, check your connection and Apple Account, then retry or use Restore Purchases. For billing or refund requests, visit Apple’s purchase support.

Can I recover local progress after reinstalling?

Preferences and gameplay saves are stored locally and may be removed when the app is deleted. Restoring purchases is separate from restoring a local save. Keep the existing installation if you are troubleshooting missing progress.

Gameplay and privacy

How do I learn the controls?

Choose How to Play and start the interactive tutorial. It introduces movement, attacks, shields and combos. You can adjust CPU difficulty and enabled items before a match.

How do I report a bug?

Describe the fighter, arena, actions just before the problem and whether it repeats. A screenshot or short recording can help; remove personal information first. Contact us through the channel above.

How do I change analytics sharing?

Open Settings → Privacy and choose Don’t Share or Share & Improve. Sharing gameplay statistics and crash reports is optional. See the privacy policy for details.
