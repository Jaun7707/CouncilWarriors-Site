A GAME REBORN

Small arenas.
Big personalities.

Council Warriors is a colorful solo platform fighter, originally created by two brothers and being rebuilt for modern iOS.

The next chapter is in development.

Battle one or two computer opponents, learn combos and make every match your own. The upcoming version starts with four fighters and two arenas, with one permanent Full Game purchase for all 24 fighters and ten launch arenas.

No advertisements. No subscriptions. No character-by-character purchases.
